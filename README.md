# 🌱 eatGrow — Find Healthy Food Near You

## ✨ Features

- **📍 Location-Based Selection:** Choose your city and local area to find nearby produce suppliers and markets.
- **🎯 Dynamic Nutrition & Budget Filtering:**
  - **Max Price (₹/kg):** Filter produce to fit your budget.
  - **Min Protein (g/100g):** Target high-protein plant options.
  - **Max Calories (kcal/100g):** Control your calorie intake.
- **🏷️ Automated Highlighting Badges:** Dynamic tags identifying `HIGH PROTEIN`, `LOW CAL`, `BUDGET PICK`, or `FRESH PICK` based on nutritional metrics.
- **🔄 Produce Alternatives:** Quick recommendations for similar produce items for easy substitutions.
- **🗺️ Integrated Map & Directions:** Click on any vendor to view an embedded satellite/map view and fetch instant directions via Google Maps.
- **📱 Responsive & Retro Visual Layout:** Built with custom CSS styling featuring a warmth-inspired crate layout, bold typography, and responsive grid design.

  - `Alfa Slab One`
